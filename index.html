\ About Us \

More than a club.
A family that plays together.

THE HAWK Elite Football Club is a youth academy in Seberang Perai Tengah, Penang, committed to developing young footballers on and off the pitch.

  • Excellence

    Structured coaching pathways from U6 through U12, built around long-term player development.

  • Community

    A supportive club culture where players, parents and coaches grow together.

  • Character

    Discipline, teamwork and sportsmanship taught alongside technical football skills.
